# Rota designs

Each screen as a PNG (`png/`) and as a standalone page you can open in a browser (`html/`). They show one example week, 9–15 November, with example people, hours and contracts: use them for layout, wording and behaviour, not as data.

| Screen | Size | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `Main` | iPad landscape | Week planner by site: shifts per site and day, open shifts (dashed, "Fill"), a clash (amber), closed days, and the problem chips above the grid. |
| `WeekByPerson` | iPad landscape | The same week by person: hours against contract, each shift with its site's name, Off, Holiday and "Can't work". |
| `ShiftEdit` | Phone | The shift sheet for an open shift, with ranked suggestions and the reason each person is or isn't a good fit. |
| `DayTimeline` | Phone | One site, one day: shift bars, people on shift per hour against the minimum, and a one-tap fix for a clash. |
| `MyShifts` | Phone | A member of staff's next shift and week, with total hours. |
| `Swaps` | Phone | Open shifts to pick up, a cover request from a colleague, and the person's own request waiting for approval. |
| `Availability` | Phone | Weekly pattern, sites they can get to, time off and its status. |
| `TodayOnShift` | Phone | The diary's Today page with "On shift today", and who opens and who closes. |
| `Hours` | Phone | Hours per person against contract, per-site totals, CSV download and approval. |

They show the original diary's look; in an improved version of the diary, follow its own design system and use these for content and behaviour. The brief (`../BRIEF.md`) is the source of truth where a design and the brief differ: for example, the phone version of the week planner (one day at a time) and Rota settings aren't drawn here.
