Privacy policy

Wholesale ordering · Last updated 24 November 2026

Who we are

This policy covers our wholesale ordering website, strangerscoffee.trade, and the accounts, orders and invoices that go with it. It's run by Strangers Coffee Company Ltd, 122 Ber Street, Norwich, NR1 3ES ("Strangers", "we"). We decide how this information is used. Questions or requests: sales@strangerscoffee.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your business name, contact name, order and accounts email addresses, phone number, username and delivery or collection addresses. Your password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form we can't read.
  • Orders: what you order, when, how it's delivered or collected, any comments and references you add, and tracking links.
  • Invoices and payments: invoices we issue you, your payment terms and whether an invoice has been paid. We never take or store card or bank details on this website.
  • Technical information: sign-in records, your IP address (used to stop repeated failed sign-ins) and error reports from the website (the page, browser and device type), so we can keep it working and secure.

How we use it

  • To run your account, take, pack and deliver your orders, and email you about them (confirmations, ready to collect, dispatched).
  • To invoice you and keep our accounts. We use QuickBooks (Intuit) for our accounting: when you're invoiced, your business name, addresses, email, order lines and invoice amounts are sent to our QuickBooks account, which may also email you the invoice and tell us when it's been paid.
  • To let our team know about new orders, including in our internal Slack channel (business name and bags only, never prices or contact details).
  • To keep the website secure and fix problems.

We do this to supply you under our agreement with you, to meet our legal duties (such as keeping tax records) and for our legitimate interest in running and protecting our business. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the website and our business, each acting on our instructions: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Resend (sending emails), Intuit QuickBooks (accounting and invoices) and Slack (our team's messages). Some of these may handle information outside the UK; where they do, they use the safeguards UK data protection law requires. We may also share information with our accountant, delivery couriers, or where the law requires it.

Cookies and your device

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. The website also saves a few things in your own browser, such as your basket, bag size and delivery choice, so they're there when you come back. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you trade with us. Orders and invoices are kept for at least six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK tax law requires. Sign-in and error records are kept for a short time only (error reports for 30 days).

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted (apart from records we must keep by law), or object to how we use it. Email sales@strangerscoffee.com. You can update most of your details yourself in My account. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes, with a new date at the top.
