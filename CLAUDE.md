# Strangers Coffee Kitchen Diary

The staff food safety diary (Safer Food, Better Business) for Strangers Coffee's four Norwich sites, at **https://staffstrangerscoffee.com**, and the staff rota, from the same Worker, at **https://rota.staffstrangerscoffee.com** (`ROTA_HOST`). This website is the only copy of the diary: it once mirrored a Claude artifact in the owner's account and synced with it hourly, but that sync has been removed and the page is now this project's own.

Read `README.md`, `README-deploy.md`, `docs/data-model.md`, `docs/runtime-api.md` and `docs/design.md` before changing anything. The person running you is the host. Ask them before anything that costs money, changes DNS, or needs a secret.

## How it fits together

- One **Cloudflare Worker** (TypeScript, no framework) with static assets from `public/`, `run_worker_first` so every request passes access control. Deployed by Workers Builds on every push to `main`.
- **D1** database `kitchen-diary`: one generic `docs` table holding every diary document by path (`docs/data-model.md`), plus accounts, sessions and rate limits. The Worker creates its own tables (`src/schema.ts`), then runs any numbered migrations not yet recorded in `schema_migrations` (`MIGRATIONS` in `src/schema.ts`: to change the schema, add one at the end; never edit one that has shipped). List queries are sorted and limited by the database (`queryStatement` in `src/docs.ts`), with indexes on the fields pages sort by (`date`, `week`, `at`); sorting by another field works but reads the whole collection, so add an index migration if it's a big one. D1 and KV have no ids in `wrangler.jsonc` because Cloudflare provisions them.
- **KV** namespace (binding `FILES`) for allergen PDFs, served at `/_blob/<id>`, and wholesale catalogue photos, served through the authenticated wholesale image routes.
- The page (`public/index.html`, `public/diary/*.js`) reaches its data only through `window.claude.use("db" | "user" | "assets" | "rota")`, provided by `public/claude-shim.js` against the Worker's `/api` routes (`docs/runtime-api.md`). Keep that boundary: the page doesn't call `fetch` for data itself.
- **Management → Fire safety log and Accident book** (docs/data-model.md): `fire` is a scheduled check list like `cleaning` (`TASKS.fire` and `FIRE_DEFAULTS` in `public/diary/records.js`, overdue checks on Today and in the summary); `accidents` is private (above), its page in `public/diary/management.js`. Tests: `accidents-fire-api.test.mjs`, `fire-accidents.e2e.test.mjs`.
- **Front-end files** (no build step): the diary's page logic is in `public/diary/`, eleven classic scripts that share one global scope, loaded by `public/index.html` in dependency order: `core.js` (constants, state `S`, helpers, writes, icons), `today.js`, `week.js`, `records.js`, `maintenance.js` (with the notifications bell), `management.js`, `allergens.js`, `reports.js` (PDFs, EHO inspection mode), `shell.js` (navigation and account), `setup.js`, then `start.js` (tabs, subscriptions, events, start-up). Code that runs as a file loads may only use what earlier files (or earlier lines) define; functions are called later freely. `public/shared/core.js` holds the helpers all three apps share (`window.SC`: `el`, `svgi`, toasts, Europe/London dates, sign-in redirect, the `pageshow` session check, the `/api/changes` watcher) and loads first on each page; the rota (`public/rota/rota.js`) and admin (`public/admin/admin.js`) take what they need from it. `isRotaSharedFile`, `isAdminSharedFile` and `isPublicCode` (`src/hosts.ts`) let each host serve it.
- Styles: `public/app.css` (base), `public/theme.css` (Liquid Glass tokens and materials), `public/ui.css` (app layout). See `docs/design.md`.
- **The rota** is a second app chosen by hostname (`appForHost` in `src/hosts.ts`): on `ROTA_HOST` the Worker serves `public/rota/` at `/`, the sign-in page, `/api/auth/*`, `/api/rota/*` and the shared styles, redirects diary pages to `/` and refuses the diary's data APIs; on any other host the diary is unchanged plus `/api/rota/*` and `/rota/` (for local dev). The rota's data is in its own `rota_*` tables (`docs/data-model.md`), its API in `src/rota/` (the router in `index.ts`, the checks in `problems.ts`, the routes by area in `routes/`). The rota app calls `/api/rota/*` with fetch, not through the shim; the diary reaches the rota only through `use("rota")` (Today's "On shift today" and who opens and closes).

## Access

Authentication is centralized in `authorize()` (`src/access.ts`) and `src/auth.ts`; employee authorization is enforced in `src/employeePrivacy.ts`, `src/rota/privacy.ts` and the routes as well. Never treat a visible/hidden menu, the generic business-manager helper or the legacy rota capability name as permission to read employee data. Admin account APIs remain in `src/admin.ts` and `src/adminView.ts`.
- **The admin section** is a third app chosen by hostname: on `ADMIN_HOST` (`admin.staffstrangerscoffee.com`) the Worker serves `public/admin/` at `/`, `/api/admin/*`, `/api/rota/*`, `/api/auth/*` and the shared theme, to admins only (everyone else is sent to the diary; the API returns 403) and refuses the diary's data APIs. `/admin` on the diary forwards there; with no `ADMIN_HOST` (local dev, tests) it's served at `/admin`. It covers the diary and the rota: overview, people (accounts, availability, training), sites, activity, security and exports. Account events, lockouts, failed sign-ins and hand-made backups are logged in `audit_log` (`src/audit.ts`). On the Overview, notices about things that happened (errors, failed emails, a device's clock, a site closed today) can be dismissed for every admin (`admin_dismissed`); errors and failed emails come back when there's a new one. The page builds HTML only through its escaping `html` template (`public/admin/admin.js`).
- One sign-in page, **Staff Login**, on the diary's address: on any other subdomain of `COOKIE_DOMAIN` (the rota), `/signin` redirects there with `next=` back (`unifiedSignin` in `src/hosts.ts`). Signed out, only `/signin` and its files, and the diary's and rota's scripts, styles and images (code, no data: `isPublicCode` in `src/hosts.ts`), are reachable; every page redirects to sign-in and the API returns 401 `unauthenticated` (the shim then sends the person to sign in). The code loads signed out because Safari can show a page from memory after the session has ended; with its script it moves on to sign-in instead of showing unstyled and stuck. Each app also re-checks the session on `pageshow`.
- Username + password (8+ characters). Each account also has a display **name**, shown on records. Admins give out temporary passwords; people choose their own at first sign-in. Five wrong passwords lock an account for 10 minutes; each IP is limited to 30 failed attempts per 15 minutes. Each attempt is counted before the password or PIN is checked (`takeAccountAttempt`, `takeIpAttempt` in `src/auth.ts`), so attempts sent at once can't get past either limit. Writes to `/api/*` from another site's page (a foreign `Origin`) are refused.
- Sessions are `HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax` cookies on `Domain=COOKIE_DOMAIN` (`staffstrangerscoffee.com`, so one sign-in covers the diary and the rota; after migration 14, old sessions require fresh password sign-in and `GET /api/auth/me` remains read-only), HMAC-signed with a key the Worker generates and keeps in D1, valid 30 days. A new password, renaming, switching off or "sign out everywhere" ends all of that person's sessions; signing out ends just that one on the server too (its id goes in `revoked_sessions` until it would have expired), so a copied cookie stops working.
- On the rota, only admins plan/publish, assign shifts, amend actual hours, see full candidate eligibility/hours/CSV and approve employee requests. Staff and managers see only their own assigned published shifts/private information plus scrubbed unassigned opportunities. Managers retain a separate business site-settings capability (opening times/minimum cover), not employee administration. Only admins set contracts, days and sites an employee can work; employees read their own availability.
- Roles: **staff**, **manager** (also business EHO, HACCP & risk, suppliers, approved shared contacts/onboarding and Setup) and **admin** (also employee administration and Admin). Managers are nonadmins for every private employee field. Training is admin-or-owner; contacts/onboarding use explicit admin-controlled shared-business/private classification. Nonadmins cannot change ownership/classification or learn another employee’s current record from an error. Business write denials use `permission_denied`, never `invalid_argument` as a role signal.
- The first account is created on the sign-in page with the **setup code**: the `SETUP_CODE` secret (`SYNC_TOKEN`, its old name, still works). The same code lets an admin who forgot their password set a new one.
- Records retain `web:<name>` attribution and old `u_…` labels from `config/people`. Those IDs, names, signatures, `personId` and document IDs must never establish employee ownership. Stable `users.id` is exposed as `userId` by the diary shim and held in `S.myUserId`; admin team data includes stable IDs/active state for linking records, including inactive accounts.
- **Quick switching**: staff and managers may set a 4-digit quick PIN and switch to themselves with it, but only on a device that has a signed `sfbb_device` cookie from a password sign-in (180 days), tied to that person and their `session_ver`, so a new password, switching them off or "sign out everywhere" ends the device's trust. Admins never use PINs. Wrong PINs share the password lockout, and the lock also switches the PIN off (so it can't be guessed 5 tries at a time). An admin password reset clears the PIN.
- **Diary records**: staff can't delete `days`, `weeks` or `reviews` documents (`requireDelete`), and a staff member's signature (`signed:{by,name}`, a review's `by`) must be their own unless it's unchanged (`requireOwnSignature`, both in `src/access.ts`). A `set` on an existing day or week page merges like `update` (`setOrMergeDoc` in `src/docs.ts`). Only managers and admins undo a sign-off (`signed: null` on a day or week), reopen a closed day or close an earlier one; staff mark only today closed, in their own name with a reason (`requireStaffDayRules`), and Admin → Overview flags it.
- **Headers**: a Content-Security-Policy allows only the site's own scripts and connections (`withSecurityHeaders` in `src/index.ts`): no inline scripts or event-handler attributes in pages.
- **Speed**: pages link their own scripts and stylesheets by fingerprint (`/diary/core.js?v=<hash>`, `withFingerprints` in `src/index.ts`, from each file's ETag), and a fingerprinted file is kept by the browser for a year, so opening a page again fetches none of them and a deploy still reaches everyone at once. Link new scripts and stylesheets as plain `/path.js` in the HTML: the Worker adds the fingerprint. Reads (GETs and `/api/db/get|query|poll|profiles`) are retried once if the database drops out for a moment (`isTransient`).
- **Errors**: every page loads `/errors.js` first, which reports script errors, unhandled rejections and files that didn't load to `POST /api/errors` (signed in or not; rate-limited; it answers with the error's code); the Worker records its own unexpected 500s too (`recordError` in `src/errors.ts`). One row per error and page with a count, kept 30 days, shown in Admin → Activity → Errors and flagged on the Overview when recent. Errors that are expected and already shown to the person (offline, signed out, refused saves) aren't reported, nor is anything from another address (browser extensions, Cloudflare's analytics beacon, which the CSP blocks on purpose); a request that failed because the signal dropped and nothing caught is reported but shows no card. Each error has a code, `E-` and six characters, the same every time it happens (`errorCode` in `src/errors.ts`, worked out the same way in `public/errors.js`, so a test checks they agree). When it's a real fault, the page shows a card with the code, what went wrong, where in the code and when (`#sc-error`, styled inline because a missing stylesheet may be the fault); a server 500's message names its code (`ref`); and Admin → Errors → Report lays out everything about it to screenshot or copy.
- **Fixing an error from a screenshot** (for Claude): the code (`E-…`) identifies the error; the report's `Where:` line is the first frame in the site's own code (`/diary/today.js:120:7 in renderDay`). Line numbers are for the version that was live at the time shown (UK time), so read that version, not the current one: `git log -1 --format=%H --before="<that time, as UTC>" origin/main`, then `git show <sha>:public/diary/today.js | sed -n 115,125p`. A screenshot of the staff card has the code, message, place and time but not the stack; the admin report has the stack, the device and how many times it happened. To reproduce, the API test for errors and `test/errors.e2e.test.mjs` show how to report one.
- **Rota emails** (`src/email/`): only the recipient’s permitted assignments, own hours/contract and calendar data; no co-worker names, counterpart shift snapshots or personal-account Reply-To. Requester/taker notices show only their own permitted change; declined cover takers receive no assigned-other date/time. Managers’ Monday summaries contain business safety information only, without employee training counts, private requests/leave or assigned staffing totals; admins retain the full summary. Each person manages their own preferences, managers/admins their own summary sites. Mock Resend with `RESEND_API_URL`; never send production mail during tests.
- **Security tests**: cover direct requests as well as hidden UI, including managers trying another employee’s read/write, query/poll/delta and conflict output, hours/CSV, requests, email HTML/text/headers/calendar, EHO/PDF, backups and stale shared-device caches. Admin and stable-owner positive cases must remain. Test role/ownership changes and legacy forged metadata; do not infer privacy from only a hidden menu.
- **Several devices at once** (docs/runtime-api.md, "Changes from several devices"): every write returns a version, and whole-document saves carry the version they were based on (`ifVersion`), so a change from an older copy gets 409 `conflict` instead of overwriting; editors merge non-overlapping edits by themselves (`saveEdited` in `public/diary/core.js`). Lists already on a device are kept up to date with only what changed (`delta` in `poll`, `src/docs.ts`; merged by the shim), and the diary loads its long lists (days, weeks, deliveries, Prove it) short at first and in full only when a screen needs older records (`needHistory` in `public/diary/start.js`); every site at a glance comes from one live summary (`src/summary.ts`, `db.summary` in the shim), with the site on screen using its own lists (docs/runtime-api.md, "Live updates"). Logs change through list operations (`$op` prepend/remove/max, `src/paths.ts`), never as whole arrays. Changes are pushed: every page keeps a WebSocket to `GET /api/live` (a hibernating Durable Object, `src/live.ts`; `SC.live`), which is sent the change counters after each write, and polls only once a minute while it's open (every few seconds when it isn't). The rota and admin pages check `GET /api/changes` and redraw when another device changes something; shift edits carry `ifUpdatedAt`. Write limits are per person.
- **Time**: the whole site runs on the server's clock in UK time (Europe/London), never the device's. Every response the browser never keeps (`no-store`: the API and pages) carries `x-now`, and pages learn the time only from those: a kept file (the logo, for a day) would come back instantly with the time it was first fetched and put the diary on yesterday; `public/shared/core.js` (loaded first on every page) works out how far the device's clock is off and replaces `Date` so `Date.now()` and `new Date()` give the server's time everywhere (remembered offline in localStorage `sc.clock`); the diary's "today" is `SC.londonToday()`, a saved time's day is `dayOf(ts)` and times show in UK time (`timeZone: "Europe/London"`). Write new code the same way: never take a date from a device-local `getHours()`/`getDate()` of now or of a timestamp. The server sets any `at`-type time more than 5 minutes in the future to now (`clampFuture` in `src/docs.ts`, not inside `$op` list operations), and a device whose own clock is over 5 minutes out (`x-device-time`) is flagged on Admin → Overview (`clock_off` in `audit_log`). Tests that need a set time pin both the page clock and the server's (`serverTimeIs` in `test/harness.mjs`).
- **Offline/shared devices**: private UI and data/queue replay are bound to the expected stable account ID and role. Use the shared auth guard and `x-expected-user-id`/`x-expected-user-role`; a mismatch returns 409 `session_changed` before routing. Verify on focus/pageshow/auth changes and hide private cached content when the session cannot be verified. The first identity binding requires fresh asynchronous server proof; body/stream readers must verify identity before releasing data. Clear sensitive in-memory data/editors on invalidation/reassignment. New private `staff`/`contacts`/`onboarding` writes are online-only, never newly queued as plaintext. Keep existing unsent queues for their original identity, quarantined from automatic replay; never delete user work as a shortcut. Legacy queue storage is not encrypted and same-profile DevTools can inspect it; document this limit rather than claiming encryption or browser-profile isolation. Operational business queues continue.
- **Backups**: `src/backup.ts`, admin-only nightly/on-demand JSON in KV, last 30 retained. Never include password or PIN hashes. Preserve stable account IDs, top-level `documentSequence` (`meta.seq`) and every row’s `seq` plus `employee_privacy_version` together with its exact data on restore; do not manufacture verification or lower the global counter below restored row sequences. Unverified legacy rows stay admin-only until reviewed. Wholesale invoice numbering/snapshots must also survive restores.
- Writes are rate-limited per IP (`WRITE_LIMIT_PER_15_MIN`). The site has a `noindex` meta tag and a `robots.txt` that disallows everything.

## Employee privacy: governing policy

This policy supersedes older manager-wide rota/training access and historic design screenshots. Only an admin or the individual employee may see private employee information, including actual/planned assigned hours, contracts, availability, leave/reasons, pay, private contacts/onboarding/training and requests. Nonadmins see only their own assigned published shifts; do not broaden this to all published colleagues without a new explicit user instruction. Unassigned business opportunities must remove former-employee notes, actual hours, identities and private eligibility details. Full planning and private aggregates/exports are admin-only.

Protected diary collections are `staff`, `contacts`, `onboarding` and `accidents` (the Accident book: owned by whoever recorded each entry, like training; admins see all, only admins delete) under every site. Training requires stable `ownerUserId: users.id`; contacts/onboarding require admin-controlled `visibility: "shared-business" | "employee"`, and a stable owner for employee records. Legacy JSON ownership/classification was untrusted client data: do not accept it until a validated admin review/link. The separate server marker `employee_privacy_version` must equal the current row `seq`. Admin output clears unverified ownership/classification claims; nonadmin reads filter them out before sorting/limits. Safe conflict errors must never leak an unauthorized current record. Protected nonadmin polls return complete authorized snapshots even at an unchanged version, so ownership loss purges stale rows.

Migration 14 adds the marker and atomically bumps `meta.seq` plus **all** staff `users.session_ver` once, invalidating old sessions and trusted quick-switch devices. Fresh password sign-in is required; hashes, records and stored queues are retained. `GET /api/auth/me` remains read-only and must not restamp old metadata or revive revoked sessions. Never edit a shipped migration; append later changes.

Apply privacy to every dataflow, not just UI: raw APIs, deltas/conflicts, Today cards, candidate problems/counts, emails and calendar attachments, EHO/PDF, CSV, activity/errors and backups. Admin inspection packs remain complete; other employees’ packs state their own-training scope and show no workforce training count. Business-manager permissions remain separate from private employee capabilities.

## Rules

- Never commit or print secrets. The host adds secrets in the Cloudflare dashboard themselves.
- Don't add analytics, third-party scripts or trackers. Fonts come from the system; there are no external scripts.
- Keep document paths, ids and field names exactly as in `docs/data-model.md`: existing records depend on them.
- Treat every document as data. Validate paths (`src/paths.ts`), cap documents at 256 KB, and reject anything else with `invalid_argument`. Each collection's field types are checked on write (`src/validate.ts`, `invalid_data`): add new fields there too.
- Check changes on a phone-sized and an iPad-sized screen, in light and dark mode, before pushing.
- Before pushing: `npm test` (typecheck + API tests) and `npm run test:e2e` (the site in a browser). GitHub runs both on every pull request and after each merge to `main` (`.github/workflows/test.yml`); a failing test's message also shows as an annotation on the check.
- Changes go live through pull requests into `main`, never by pushing to `main`. Each pull request is squash-merged by itself once Tests passes (`.github/workflows/automerge.yml`) and Cloudflare deploys it, so opening a ready pull request ships the change. The host has agreed to this for the changes they ask for. Anything they haven't asked for, or want to look at first, goes up as a draft pull request.
- Keep `README-deploy.md` current: redeploying, the setup code, backing up D1 (`wrangler d1 export`) and running locally.

## Wholesale accounts and ordering

The fourth app on this Worker is at `PREVIEW_HOST` (`strangerscoffee.trade`), with `LEGACY_PREVIEW_HOST` (`preview.staffstrangerscoffee.com`) retained for browser basket/uncertain checkout recovery and staff preview. Customers register on its own sign-in page and must be approved by an admin before accessing ordering. `src/wholesaleAccounts.ts` owns separate accounts and host-only, opaque sessions; these must never become staff `User` identities or authorize diary/rota/Admin APIs. Passwords use the existing PBKDF2 helpers; only session-token hashes are stored. Migration 12 adds the account/session tables and a customer order index. Backups include account metadata and approval status, never credentials or sessions.

Staff retain Staff Login on the staff domain through the Staff sign-in link (returning to the legacy preview) and can save marked `PREVIEW-…` test orders. Customer orders are `WS-…`, `preview:false`, linked server-side to their business, with immutable delivery/price snapshots. Customers see only their business's orders and public statuses; never internal notes. New submissions are refused on hold. UUID request IDs remain idempotent and edits versioned. No payment gateway, automatic email or shipping integration is invoked.

Management remains the native `public/admin/wholesale.js` module in the existing Admin menu, using escaped templates and sheets. The `html` helper returns a safe object: use `put(node, html\`…\`)` to render it, never assign it directly to `innerHTML`. Admins approve/decline registrations, create/reset/disable logins, maintain customers and update orders. The ordering UI has My account, My orders and confirmation before reorder. Keep images in their intrinsic proportions; the old marketing introduction has been removed. The shared catalogue still uses sourced prices unless an admin overrides them.

`src/wholesaleCatalogue.ts` adds/edits sourced and custom coffees, prices, visibility and stock using the existing JSON records; `src/wholesaleStore.ts` shares versioned D1 access. Uploaded photos use `FILES` keys `wholesale-image:<id>` (up to six JPEG/PNG/WebP, 5 MB each). Detect the actual raster MIME type: some original `.webp` files contain JPEG bytes. Photo updates require the coffee version; failed links delete the new upload. Image reads require a session and current product reference. Keep originals: backups hold references, not KV bytes. Hidden/out-of-stock coffees must never create new orders, but exact successful retries keep their original result.

Payment terms and instructions are admin-controlled per customer (default To be agreed; listed invoice periods or Custom with instructions). Public profiles expose safe read-only terms. Checkout verifies `customerVersion` after checking for an idempotent retry and snapshots terms with delivery/prices. A later terms edit cannot rewrite an order. Customer profile updates must preserve the admin-controlled terms. No new schema migration is needed for catalogue/terms fields; never edit shipped migrations.

Tests: `test/wholesale-accounts.test.mjs`, `wholesale-api.test.mjs`, `wholesale-catalogue.test.mjs`, `wholesale.test.mjs` and phone/iPad light/dark journeys in `wholesale.e2e.test.mjs` and `wholesale-catalogue.e2e.test.mjs`; add management routes to `security.test.mjs`. Run all required checks before pushing.

The visible product name is Wholesale, without Beta badges, on the existing `PREVIEW_HOST`; retain internal paths/table/flags (including API `stage:"beta"`) for compatibility. Both customer pages load `theme.css`; never replace the shared design tokens with a separate palette. `src/wholesaleAdminLists.ts` owns indexed keyset pages, escaped server search and full-record admin stats. Keep uncertain browser checkout attempts frozen/per identity across reloads; GET the owned receipt or retry the identical UUID/payload. Do not create a new request ID after an unknown outcome. Tests include `wholesale-beta-api.test.mjs` (>200-record search/paging/stats) and `wholesale-beta.e2e.test.mjs` (lost/unsent response recovery).

Further wholesale UX: customer history is ownership-scoped on every cursor page; never reuse an admin page as a customer query. Repeat-basket confirmation refreshes current prices/availability, preserves existing basket lines and must not submit an order. Admin quick statuses preserve customerId/internal notes/snapshots and use ifVersion, with explicit refresh after conflicts. Staff previews have no quick packing actions. Regression files include `wholesale-history-api.test.mjs` and `wholesale-ux.e2e.test.mjs`.

Order deletion is admin-only and version-checked, with a native confirmation. Retain a tombstone in the existing order record for audit/idempotency, hide it from every normal read/list/count/stat, and reject original matching checkout retries with 410 instead of recreating it. Do not trigger payments/refunds. Cover DELETE in the central permission matrix and `wholesale-delete-api.test.mjs` / `wholesale-delete.e2e.test.mjs`.

Wholesale locations: preserve the permanent primary/legacy-field mapping and old omitted-location request hashes. Extra location IDs are server-generated and business-scoped; profiles/locations use CAS, and order location snapshots are immutable. Customer profile writes cannot rename the approved business. Keep pending checkout payloads frozen, including locationId. Customer grind/location pickers use native dialogs and close controls must never overlap content.

Wholesale invoicing: migration 13 adds supplier settings and immutable issued invoices; never modify shipped migrations 11/12. Keep preview separate from issue, require explicit supplier/tax configuration, and calculate money using integer pence/rate basis points. Issue is atomic against order/settings versions with one invoice per real order and monotonic unique numbers; exact retries recover, changed requests conflict. Preserve all invoice snapshots/numbering after order deletion and in backups. Manual payment metadata has independent CAS and never triggers a bank transaction. Customer invoice lists/PDFs must be business-scoped. Native PDF metadata is fixed to issuance for stable downloads. Required regressions include wholesale-invoices-api, wholesale-invoice-pdf, wholesale-invoices.e2e and wholesale-desktop.e2e; invoice routes belong in central security tests.

Customer invoices switch: Admin → Invoicing has an "Invoices for customers" switch (`PUT /api/admin/wholesale/invoice-settings/customers {ifVersion, enabled}`, stored as `customersSeeInvoices` in the invoice settings row, absent = on, kept when supplier settings are saved). Off: customer invoice routes return 403 `invoices_unavailable`, order lists carry no invoice links, session/catalogue say `invoices:false` and My orders hides the Invoices tab and balance note (re-checked each time My orders opens). Admin issuing, payment records, PDFs and QuickBooks are unaffected.

Wholesale delivery charges: `shippingFor` (`public/wholesale-preview/shipping.js`, shared by server and page) gives £7.44 including VAT for Delivery under £250 of coffee, free from £250, nothing for collection. The server sets `deliveryPence`/`totalPence` when an order is placed (clients can't send them) and they never change; the invoice form pre-fills from them. 500 g bags are gone (no catalogue price; `pack_unavailable` for new orders; past orders keep their lines). Quantities are typed or stepped, up to 999 bags of one coffee/size/grind (`MAX_QUANTITY` in `public/wholesale-preview/limits.js`, shared by checkout, invoices and the QuickBooks preview). Grinds are Beans (default), Fine (Commercial espresso machine), Medium (Filter) and Coarse (Cafetiere and batch brewers), each `hint` shown smaller and editable per coffee.
